The Mission

From the heart of Europe to the southern tip of Africa, this solo cycling expedition aims to bridge cultures, raise awareness for humanitarian causes, and inspire others to pursue their dreams. Through this journey, we're supporting local charities and documenting the diverse communities and landscapes along the way.

The Route

Starting from Brussels, Belgium, the route winds through Europe, crosses the Mediterranean, traverses the length of Africa, and finally reaches Cape Town, South Africa. Along the way, we'll pass through diverse landscapes including the Alps, the Sahara Desert, the East African savannah, and everything in between.
