The Mission

From the capital of Europe to the southern tip of Africa, this solo cycling expedition aims to bring cultures closer together, raise awareness of humanitarian causes, and inspire others to chase their dreams. Through this journey, I hope to support local charities and highlight the diverse communities and landscapes along the way.

The Route

Starting from Brussels, the road winds through Europe, crosses the Mediterranean, follows the Atlantic Ocean and the African continent, and finally reaches Cape Town, South Africa. Along the way, I will traverse various landscapes and climates, including the Pyrenees, the Sahara and Namib deserts, the humid savannas of West Africa, and much more.
