Beyond Borders, the Human Mosaic

Whether we set down our suitcases under the scorching sun of Côte d’Ivoire, in the bustling alleys of Spain, or across the endless plains of Namibia, one truth endures—as evident as the wind itself: we are both alike and profoundly unique. What sets us apart is not our differences, but the richness of our cultures, the melody of our languages, the fabric of our traditions, the infinite palette of our skin. And what could be more intoxicating than diving into the life of another? Sharing, if only for a moment, their dreams, their hopes, their laughter, and their silences?

From this intuition, a project was born simple in its essence, titanic in its execution. Simple, because its ambition can be summed up in a single word: to bring together. Complex, because it requires building bridges where borders, visible and invisible, have all too often erected walls. Imagine, for an instant, classrooms glowing with the same light, from Morocco to Ghana, from Belgium to South Africa. Young, curious faces, eager to understand, gathered each month by the magic screen of a video call. Children from Togo, Senegal, France, or Ghana, who, for the duration of an exchange, throw open the windows of their world to let in the echo of a distant voice, the reflection of an unknown smile. Hands reaching across kilometers, stories intertwining, gazes growing familiar.

If, like me, you are inspired by the values promoted by Masicorp and the Songezo Cycling Academy, then you too can take part in this adventure through a donation that will help fund essential needs such as food, school supplies, and the daily support provided on the ground.

The goal is to raise €14,000 — the equivalent in kilometers of the total distance between Brussels and Cape Town. All funds collected will be donated in full to Masicorp and the Songezo Cycling Academy upon my arrival in Cape Town on December 24th, in the presence of representatives from both organisations, partners-sponsors, members of the local community, friends, supporters, and, subject to availability, representatives of the media and government.

On this occasion, an official cheque presentation ceremony will be held to publicly announce the total amount raised throughout the journey.

Every contribution, no matter how small, is warmly appreciated.
