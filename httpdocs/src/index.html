The Mission

From the capital of Europe to the southern tip of Africa, this solo cycling expedition aims to bring cultures together, raise awareness for humanitarian causes, and inspire each of us to pursue our dreams. Through this journey, I hope to support local charities and highlight the diverse communities and landscapes along the way.

The Route

The road sets off from Brussels, cuts across Europe, crosses the Mediterranean, follows the Atlantic, then plunges southward to the tip of Africa. A journey where the rugged Pyrenees, the majestic Atlas, the scorching vastness of the Sahara, the red dunes of the Namib, and the lush greenery of West African savannas unfold one after another.

Each landscape is a page, each climate an emotion. The mountains are challenges, the deserts trials, the savannas promises. And at every step, the road becomes more than a path—it turns into a story, an encounter with the boundless world, all the way to the Cape, where the ocean halts the adventure but not the memories.

Special thanks to LUXURY AFRICA EXPERIENCES in South Africa, my very first sponsor, who didn’t hesitate for a second to support my project and my adventure.
